# Cloud sync

This page shows how to share one workspace between two machines. Cloud sync is
optional: staple works fully offline without it.

## What this page will cover

- What cloud sync is for, and what never leaves your machine.
- Connecting a workspace, and the consent each step asks for.
- Automatic sync, conflicts and claims across machines.
- Backups, disconnecting and removing your data from the service.

Until this page is written, the [CLI reference](cli.md#at-a-glance) lists the `staple cloud` commands.
